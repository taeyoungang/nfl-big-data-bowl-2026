# NFL Big Data Bowl: Expected Yards After Catch

This repo holds Next Gen Stats player tracking and PFF scouting data for 8,557 dropbacks in 122 games from weeks 1-8 of the 2021 season, plus code that models expected yards after catch, completion probability and receiver opportunity. The `data/` folder has `games.csv`, `plays.csv`, `players.csv`, `pffScoutingData.csv` and one `tracking/tracking_[gameId].csv` per game, joined on `gameId`, `playId` and `nflId`, with tracking at 10 frames per second, positions in yards and angles in degrees clockwise from the +y axis. Tracking starts about 0.5 s before the snap and stops 0.5 s after the throw, so the catch itself is never observed, and `plays.csv` leaves out about 1,150 completions that were mostly thrown at or behind the line, so findings apply to passes thrown beyond the line. Run `yac_features.py` then `yac_analysis.py` to build the features and models (outputs go to `processed/`, `figures/` and `yac_report.md`), `yac_viz.py` for case-study figures, and `animate_play.py --yac` to replay a play with per-receiver labels, while `eda.py` and `validate_data.py` produce the exploratory figures and the data checks. The scripts also read free public nflverse play-by-play from `external/play_by_play_2021.parquet`, which supplies the yards-after-catch label and the target receiver.
